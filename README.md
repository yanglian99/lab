# Mini Polytopia

A lightweight browser strategy prototype inspired by the exploration, city-building, and tactical movement loop of **The Battle of Polytopia**.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

### Goal

Outscore the rival tribe by reaching **40 score** first.

### Actions

- **Move / Attack**: Select your unit, then let it auto-move to the strongest adjacent tile.
- **Found City**: Stand on a neutral village with a unit and spend 5 stars.
- **Harvest**: Upgrade one of your cities by spending 3 stars on its resource.
- **End Turn**: Gain stars from your cities, then let the AI rival take its turn.

## Features

- Procedurally generated 8x8 terrain map with fog of war.
- Neutral villages, city ownership, stars economy, and score race.
- Simple enemy AI that expands, trains defenders, and attacks nearby units.
- Responsive single-page UI with event log and tile inspector.
