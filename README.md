# Mini Empires

A lightweight browser strategy game inspired by the economy, age-up pacing, and raid defense loop of **Age of Empires II**.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Features

- Gather food, wood, gold, and stone with assignable villagers.
- Train villagers and militia while managing population cap with houses.
- Build farms and towers to improve your economy and defense.
- Advance from the Dark Age to the Imperial Age.
- Survive scaling enemy raids and complete victory objectives.
