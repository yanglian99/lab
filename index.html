StarForge Command

A compact RTS inspired by StarCraft: harvest crystals, build troops, and survive the swarm.

Objectives

  • Mine crystals with drones.
  • Train marines and medics.
  • Destroy the enemy hive before your command core falls.

How to play

  • Click a unit to select it.
  • Right-click the battlefield to move selected units.
  • Drones auto-harvest nearby crystal patches.
  • Use the command panel to train reinforcements.
