World 1-1 Inspired Canvas Platformer

A complete JavaScript + HTML5 Canvas platformer with scrolling, enemies, power-ups, a bonus room, score, lives, timer, and an end-level flag.

Controls:
  • ← / → or A / D: Move
  • Space, W, or ↑: Jump
  • S / ↓: Enter pipe when standing on the enterable pipe
  • R: Restart after game over or victory
