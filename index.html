Island Siege

A compact browser strategy game inspired by amphibious raids: land troops, unlock reinforcements, and destroy the enemy HQ before your squad is wiped out.

Mission

Click the battlefield to drop troops on the beach. Troops auto-advance and attack nearby targets. Use artillery to soften defenses and medkits to keep your push alive.

Abilities

Artillery and medkits target the next click on the battlefield. Troops only deploy onto sandy beach zones.

Battle Log

    Goal: Destroy the HQ at the far side of the island. Tip: Knock out cannons first so your infantry can survive the approach.
