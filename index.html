Arcade classic

Ping Pong Game

Move your paddle, rally against the computer, and be the first to score 7 points.

You 0
Computer 0

Press Start Game to begin.

How to play

  • Move your mouse or drag on the board to control the left paddle.
  • Use W/S or ↑/↓ on your keyboard.
  • First player to 7 wins the match.
